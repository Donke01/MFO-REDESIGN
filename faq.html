Questions

Quick answers about giving and about our work. Can't find yours? Call 602‑616‑5557.

Giving

Is my gift tax deductible?

Yes. Missions for Orphans is a 501(c)(3) non‑profit, and all donations are tax deductible. The giving form asks for your billing address so we can mail your records for tax purposes at the end of the year.

How much of my gift goes to the work?

All of it. We run only on donations, with no admin costs: every gift goes to the programs. Our directors live in Kenya.

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Choose orphan care, widows, community outreach, onsite projects, or where it's needed most on the donate page.

Can I give every month?

Yes. On any giving form, choose an amount and tick “Make this donation every month”. See how.

Can I give by check or by phone?

Yes. Make a check payable to Missions for Orphans and mail it to 2497 N. Delaney Dr., Buckeye, AZ 85396, or call 602‑616‑5557 and we'll help you give.

Can I send clothes or supplies?

Clothing for all ages is always needed, for our boys and the village children. Get in touch to arrange it.

What does a gift buy?

A few real costs from our reports: $160 buys a wheelchair, $165 filled 12 baskets of essentials, $100 pays a year of high school, and in 2023 each new widow's home cost $900.

Our work

Where is Missions for Orphans?

Our home is in Kipsaina, near Kitale, in Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. Our U.S. office is in Buckeye, Arizona.

Who runs it?

Lani Orosco started Missions for Orphans in 2008 with her late husband, Coney, and now runs it with her son and daughter. In Kipsaina she is Mama Lani. Meet the family.

How are the widows helped?

About 600 widows in six village groups. We visit two groups at a time, every other week, with maize flour and beans, and a kilo of sugar at Christmas. We also build new homes.

How do I know what my gift did?

Every mission is reported with dates and photos on the Latest missions page.

How can I follow along?

Read Lani's blog, or follow her own blog to get new posts by email.
