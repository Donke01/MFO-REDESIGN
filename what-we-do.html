Fighting hunger and poverty in Trans Nzoia

Since 2008 we have worked to reduce poverty for children, orphans and their communities in rural Kenya.

Give today Or call 602-616-5557

The boys

Fifteen orphaned boys live in our home in Kipsaina under the care of dedicated house parents.

  • Daily meals, clothing and a safe place to sleep
  • Schooling and life skills
  • Faith formation and mentoring

The widows

We reach widows from more than eight tribes in the surrounding villages.

  • Food distributions every six weeks
  • Hygiene supplies and care baskets
  • Small homes with tin roofs, with a goal of six new homes a year

Village members

Lasting change comes from local jobs and local leadership.

  • 44 people employed, from teachers and pastors to guards and builders
  • Three preschools in Pokot villages
  • A 6-acre farm that supports several families
  • Sewing machines for seamstress graduates

Projects

Building work keeps the boys safe and gives widows a secure home.

  • A perimeter wall around the Kipsaina home
  • A home for Monica, a mother of nine, plus monthly food
  • Two-room 15×20 homes for widows in severe need

Medical missions

Our medical teams treat common, preventable illness in villages with little access to care.

  • Jigger removal and treatment
  • Malaria and typhoid
  • Stomach viruses

Outreach

We back children’s education and the local church.

  • Uniforms, backpacks, shoes and school fees
  • More than six churches built, plus Bibles and music equipment
  • Bi-weekly online teaching and support for conferences
  • Prison visits with food, sanitary supplies and ministry
