What we do

We work to lessen the effects of poverty on children, orphans and families in Trans Nzoia County, Kenya: hunger, poor housing, missing school and missing medical care.

The boys

In Kipsaina we care for 15 orphan boys, meeting their basic needs and giving them the life skills and faith to overcome poverty. With dedicated house parents, every boy has clothing, food, shelter and the love of a family.

We aim for more than survival: education and guidance that prepare them for a future full of opportunity.

15 boys, 2 house parents

The widows

We bring food and medical outreach to over eight tribes in the surrounding villages. Widows face food shortages, poor shelter and a lack of hygiene supplies.

We build small homes with tin roofs, hand out food every six weeks, and give essential supplies to those most in need.

Food every 6 weeks

Village members

We employ 44 people in Kipsaina and other Kenyan counties: teachers, laborers, pastors, guards and trade school students. Three preschools in Pokot villages offer education, food and jobs.

Seamstress graduates receive their own sewing machines, skilled laborers learn trades, and our 6-acre farm supports several families.

44 jobs in Kenya

Our projects

We are building a perimeter wall to keep the children and farm workers safe. We built a home for Monica, a mother of nine abandoned by her husband, furnished it with beds and blankets, and bring her food each month.

Each year we aim to build six homes for widows in the greatest need.

6 widow homes a year

Education and medical care

Many families cannot afford school, so we provide uniforms, backpacks, shoes and fees. Now far fewer children are left out of class.

Clothing is always needed, for our boys and the village children. Our medical missions treat jiggers, stomach viruses, malaria and typhoid.

Uniforms, fees and clinics

Outreach

We follow the Great Commission of Matthew 28:19-20. We've built over six churches and support many more, with internet teachings every other week, men's and women's conferences, Bibles and music equipment.

We visit the local prisons regularly with ministry, food and sanitary supplies.
