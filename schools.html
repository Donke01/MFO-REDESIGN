Schools and training

There is no free school here. We pay fees, buy uniforms and shoes, run preschools, and help young people learn a trade.

What school costs in Kipsaina

$3–$5per child, per quarter, for primary school
$100per student, per year, for high school
$500–$700per year for college, which here means trade school
$250for a year of tailoring school, sewing machine included

From Mama Lani's blog, summer 2023.

Village children in class

Most village children are raised by their grandparents, and when there is no money, a child stays home to look after the animals. We help pay primary school fees for many of them, sometimes up to 20 children, and buy their uniforms and school shoes.

In 2021, for the first time, all the village children in the area were in school.

Preschools in Pokot

We start and run preschools in Pokot villages. Our three preschools offer education, food and jobs.

The tailoring school

Our tailor, Rose, teaches for a full year: sewing, and also counsel, a mother's care and sometimes a safe place to live.

Each student gets a pedal sewing machine, which needs no electricity, and keeps it when they graduate. Fabric, thread, scissors, needles, machine oil and pattern paper are all included. In 2023 we held our sixth graduation.
