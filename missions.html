Latest missions

What your donations did, mission by mission, newest first. Tap a photo to see it full size.

April 2026

190 widows fed

On April 15th, Missions for Orphans, led by Pastor Jim and Mike, served two groups of widows from Kaisagat and Bwai.

In total, 190 women received maize flour and beans to help sustain their households. It may seem simple, but moments like these carry real weight for the families who depend on them.

We're grateful to play even a small part in supporting these women, and thankful for the hands and hearts that made it possible.

January 2026

10 wheelchairs

On January 23, 2026, we visited St. Lilian School for Physically Challenged Children and gave 10 wheelchairs to children in need, helping them move more freely and comfortably every day.

Each wheelchair costs $160, $1,600 in all. The need is still great: many more children are waiting. With your help, we can reach them.

December 2025

506 widows served

On December 16, 2025, we served 506 widows across six communities: Wiyeta, Munyaka, Karaus, Kwayu, Bwai and Kaisagat. Each woman received 1 kg of sugar and tea leaves.

Together they prayed for Missions for Orphans, asking God to expand our reach so we can keep serving those in need.

December 2025

1 new home

Meet Mary Esekhon, a widow of 27 years and mother of seven. After the heartbreaking loss of three of her children, she cares for her four remaining children with strength and grace.

Thanks to our supporters, Mary's new home is in progress: a safe, lasting place for her family. We'll share more as it goes up.

August 2025

$165 for 12 baskets

It's basket time again! We put together 12 baskets of everyday essentials for the mamas in need around our property: simple things that make a hard week a little lighter.

The total came to just $165, proof that small amounts make a big difference.

August 2025

2 rooms in every new home

We looked back on our journey of building homes for widows and saw how far it has come: from simple 8 by 8 foot spaces to today’s 15 by 20 foot homes with two rooms.

What started as a check on past builds became a reminder of what these homes mean to the families living in them.
